## Apify Platform Usage
The CLI invokes the Apify YouTube Scraper actor (`streamers~youtube-scraper`) on the official Apify cloud platform ([apify.com](https://apify.com)):
- **Free Plan**: Apify includes a monthly \$5 free credit on free accounts, allowing thousands of YouTube video scrapes without billing.
- **Paid Apify Tiers**: Higher volume scraping is billed directly by Apify based on compute units and proxy usage.

For official Apify platform subscription details and rate cards, visit [https://apify.com/pricing](https://apify.com/pricing).
